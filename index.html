What's WECEHacks 2027?

Presented by Women in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, join us for a two-day event filled with hardware, software, and cybersecurity challenges! Attend hands-on workshops, build something new with your team, and compete for prizes. All majors and genders are welcome! Stay tuned for registration.
